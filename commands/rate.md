---
description: Split a big job into installments with a spending cap per installment
argument-hint: [what the job is]
---
The user wants to do this job in installments: $ARGUMENTS

1. If the job is unclear, ask what it is and which folder it works in.
2. Estimate the WORK of each level (how many levels goes with the size, as the quote protocol says), then let Quotient work the numbers out: `sh "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/run.sh" quote-table --kind rate --raw essential=<n> good=<n> max=<n> --lang <it|en>`. Copy its table into your message as it is (each level with its time and share of the week; an installment re-reads only its own start, and the cap already pays for it), then open the choice window (AskUserQuestion), in the user's language, with two questions: 'Livello'/'Level' (one option per level, as in the table) and 'Ritmo'/'Pace' (the table's plans in days, with the amount a day and the time). The window shows 4 options at most and adds a free field by itself: say what it is for (a mix of levels; a pace of their own: how many days, how much a day, at what time, e.g. '5 giorni alle 2:00').
3. Write the job description to a file, then create the job:
   `sh "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/run.sh" rate new <short-name> --dir <folder> --task-file <file> --quote <raw estimate> --days <n>` (or `--daily <n>` for a pace per day; a pace written in the free field goes as it is with `--pace "<their words>"`, and `--at HH:MM` sets the time). A level written in the free field: estimate it and use that as `--quote`. For a book, a research or a code review add `--template book`, `--template research` or `--template code-review`: a tested way of working, with the PROGRESS line that tells how much remains (`rate templates` lists them).
4. Open a second window: 'Prima rata'/'First installment' (now; today at a time they write; tonight at the time of the plan) and 'Ogni giorno'/'Every day' (the daily time, free field; leave it out when the pace already gave the time). Then run what they chose: `rate run <name>` in the background, `rate once <name> --time HH:MM`, `rate schedule <name>` (with `--time HH:MM`, or without it for the time of the plan). Scheduling creates a system task: it is the user's choice in the window that allows it.
5. Tell the user in one line: after each installment Windows shows a notification, and the full report comes back to this chat at their next message here (`rate here <name>` in another chat moves it there).
